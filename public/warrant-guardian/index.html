The problem

Automation doesn't get tired. It also doesn't get scared.

Most people who automate their trading start by writing down their rules. Maximum loss for the day. Maximum size in one position. Maximum number of entries. The rules are usually sensible, because they were written calmly.

Then the code runs. And code has no instinct for "this has gone far enough." A strategy that is having a bad morning will keep going with exactly the same confidence it had at nine o'clock. The limits you wrote down live in a document, or in your head — not in the execution path.

Warrant Guardian's only job is to move those limits out of your head and into something that watches, continuously, and speaks up first.

What Guardian does

Some of this works today. Some is being built.

Every card below says which. We would rather show you the line than let you assume it isn't there.

01 — WATCHES WORKING TODAY

Five limits, against your live positions

Daily loss, loss on a single trade, position quantity, a consecutive-losing-trades halt, and a square-off boundary. Guardian reads your live positions from your broker account and evaluates every limit against them. Each threshold is a number you typed in.

02 — EVALUATES WORKING TODAY

Four states, and one of them is "I don't know"

Every limit resolves to OK, WARN, BREACH or UNKNOWN. WARN is a distinct state that exists before a breach, not a note filed after one.

03 — SAYS UNKNOWN WORKING TODAY

It does not guess

One unreadable row in your trade file means no losing-streak figure at all — not a figure computed from the rows that happened to parse. When Guardian cannot tell, that is what it reports.

04 — RECONSTRUCTS WORKING TODAY

Your round-trips, FIFO

Guardian rebuilds your completed round-trips from your own trade records, first in first out, and computes the losing streak from them.

05 — ALERTS PLANNED — BUILT, NOT SWITCHED ON

A warning that reaches you

Today Guardian computes a warning state. It does not yet deliver one. Until it does, nothing on this page claims that Guardian will tell you anything — you would have to look.

06 — RECORDS PLANNED

Guardian's own audit trail

Every limit you set, every warning raised, every breach, and every override — kept for your own review and your own record-keeping, with export. Not built yet. The round-trip reconstruction in card 04 is a different thing.

The boundary

What Guardian will never do

This list is not a disclaimer bolted on at the end. It is the product definition. If any of these ever changes, it changes here first, in public.

  • Never tells you what to trade. No instruments, no strikes, no entries, no exits, no signals, no tips, no watchlists.
  • Never predicts a market. There is no forecast anywhere in this product. Guardian has no opinion about tomorrow.
  • Never claims or implies a profit, a return, a win rate, or performance of any kind. Not in the product, not on this page, not in any email we send you.
  • Never places a trade — and the build enforces it. Guardian cannot place, modify or cancel an order, and our build fails if order-placing code appears in it. Being straight about that test: it matches known order-method names, so it would not catch a raw HTTP call to an order endpoint. We are hardening it to cover outbound destinations too.
  • Never gives investment advice. Nothing Guardian outputs is a recommendation, and nothing here should be read as one.
  • Never hides its reasoning. No score you can't trace, no model you can't inspect, no "our algorithm determined."

White-box

Read every line.

A risk tool you can't inspect is just another black box in a stack that already has too many. Every rule Guardian applies is a rule you can open and read. When a limit approaches the threshold you set, that surfaces as an explicit WARN state — not a score you have to take on trust.

# Illustrative — the shape of a Guardian warning. # Not a real alert, and not a suggestion to do anything. LIMIT daily_loss_ceiling SET BY you, on <the date you set it> THRESHOLD <the figure you entered> CURRENT <where you are now> STATE APPROACHING ACTION none taken — Guardian warns, you decide

No figures are shown above because we won't put invented numbers on a page about trustworthiness. In the beta you'll see your own.
